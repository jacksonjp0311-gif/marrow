# Marrow

Continuous field.

## Purpose

Keep a persistent geometry that the runners can read from and write into.
Awake does the work. Sleep maintains the field.

## Limits

- The field grows only by append.
- Readout is fixed size.
- Changes that alter the field or the runners remain candidates until the gate passes and twin control shows a measured difference.
- Durable promotion requires human authorize.

## Witness

Everything is recorded. The log is append-only.

We keep the field. We do not pretend it thinks.
